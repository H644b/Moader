# Seedsow on-console (standalone)

This method uses a computer once to prepare the SD card and the Seedsow profile save. Later boots run on the Switch.

## Requirements

- Seedsow Lullaby and a save for the edition and profile you use.
- A computer for initial setup and the [v3.0.0 backend kit](https://github.com/H644b/Moader/releases/download/v3.0.0/Moader-backend-v3.0.0.zip).
- A matching filesystem capture from your own setup for in-game mods.

The console check used the Demo. The full game still needs verification.

## Installation

1. Extract the backend kit on your computer. In the published v3.0.0 download, the standalone guide for SD card and save preparation is `docs/SEEDSOW_STANDALONE.md`.
2. After setup, confirm that the [required app and plugin](INSTALLATION.md#install-the-app-and-plugin) are on your SD card.
3. Open **Moader** from **hbmenu** and [verify the runtime connection](INSTALLATION.md#check-your-installation).

After a full reboot, use the prepared SD card and the same Seedsow profile. Your Moader library stays on the card.
