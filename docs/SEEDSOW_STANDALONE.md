# Seedsow on-console (standalone)

You need a computer once to prepare your SD card and Seedsow profile save. After that, you can start Moader from the Switch after each full reboot.

1. Download the [v3.0.0 backend kit](https://github.com/H644b/Moader/releases/download/v3.0.0/Moader-backend-v3.0.0.zip) on your computer.
2. In that download, open `docs/SEEDSOW_STANDALONE.md` for your edition. In-game mods require a matching filesystem capture from your own setup.
3. Open **Moader** from **hbmenu** and [check the connection](GETTING_STARTED.md#check-your-installation).

The console check used the Demo. The full game still needs verification.
