# Quick answers

**Why is my library empty?** Games appear after you [add a mod](USAGE.md#upload-your-first-mod).

**What happens after a reboot?** Your mods and settings stay on the SD card. Start [your setup method](GETTING_STARTED.md#start-the-backend-for-this-boot) again to use mods in games.

**Can I close Moader?** The browser stays available if the resident service is running. Otherwise, keep the app open while using the browser.

**Why is Store gray?** The Store is disabled in this release. Download a mod ZIP from its publisher and use **Upload mod**.
