# SmileBASIC

You'll need SmileBASIC on your Switch and a computer after each full reboot.

1. On your computer, download and unzip the [Moader backend kit](https://github.com/H644b/Moader/releases/download/v3.0.0/Moader-backend-v3.0.0.zip).
2. Open `docs/INSTALLATION.md` in the v3.0.0 kit. Mac users can choose **mac2jb** or **direct ns2jb**. On Linux or Windows with WSL2, choose **direct ns2jb**.
3. After setup, open **Moader** from **hbmenu**. Press the left stick, then open **Settings → Advanced → Runtime details**.

When **Runtime connection** says **Connected**, you can [add your first mod](USAGE.md#upload-your-first-mod).
