# Moader v3.5.0 — Community Store

Browse the community catalog from the **Store** on your Switch or in its paired browser. Search for a mod, open its screenshots and release details, then choose **Download & install**. The download is checked against its published SHA-256 before it reaches your Library. Enable the mod when you're ready.

**On this Switch** includes games identified through the HOME menu, game cards, play history, saves and your Moader Library. Recognized physical titles stay in the filter when you swap cards. You can also remove a mod from the Library and reinstall it using the same ID.

## Update

1. Close Moader and download [Moader-v3.5.0.zip](https://github.com/H644b/Moader/releases/download/v3.5.0/Moader-v3.5.0.zip).
2. Copy both folders to the SD card. The ZIP places the app at `switch/Moader.nro` and the service at `kaihen/plugins/moader.nso`.
3. Open Moader. If the previous service is running, choose **Settings → Advanced → Reload Moader service**. Refresh the paired browser.

The v3.0.0 backend is compatible with this app update. [Installation guide](../INSTALLATION.md) · [Store guide](../USAGE.md#community-mods) · [Community catalog](https://moader.timsfun.com/)
