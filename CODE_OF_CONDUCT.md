# Community conduct

Moader welcomes people learning homebrew as well as experienced contributors. Be considerate, explain technical disagreements clearly and help others reproduce a problem without belittling them.

Respect privacy and consent. Do not post another person's identifying information, credentials, device/account data or private messages. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation and repeated unwanted contact are not welcome. Do not use issues or pull requests to distribute stolen code, keys or copyrighted game assets.

Keep discussions relevant and constructive. A maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Decisions should reflect the conduct and its impact, rather than technical experience or agreement with a maintainer.

For a conduct concern, contact **@H644b** through a private channel available to you. If you have no such channel, open a minimal issue asking how to contact the maintainer safely; do not include sensitive details in that issue. Security vulnerabilities belong in the [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and to interactions where someone represents the project. It does not promise round-the-clock moderation or a particular response time.
